COVARIANCE AND CORRELATION OUTPUTS\n\nnqpca_covariance_matrix.csv: 15x15 covariance matrix computed from globally standardized features Xz, matching C15 = cov(Xz) in main_NQPCA.m.\nnqpca_correlation_matrix.csv: 15x15 Pearson correlation matrix across the 15 original feature columns.\n08_covariance_matrix.png and 08_correlation_matrix.png: heatmap visualizations; axes list the feature names and each cell is annotated with its value rounded to two decimals. CSV files retain eight decimal places.\n\nInterpretation: covariance diagonal values are approximately 1 because features were standardized; off-diagonal covariance values show joint variation. Correlation ranges from -1 to +1: positive means features tend to increase together, negative means one tends to increase as the other decreases, and near zero means weak linear relationship. Correlation is scale-free.\n
